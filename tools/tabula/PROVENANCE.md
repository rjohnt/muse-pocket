# Provenance of the Tabula plates

Generated by `tools/tabula/build_plates.py report`. Do not edit.

Jacques Callot (1592–1635), *Les Images de tous les Saincts et Saintes de l'Année*, etchings published by Israël Henriet, Paris, 1636. The impressions are in the Department of Drawings and Prints of The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York, which released the images under [Creative Commons Zero](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) through its Open Access programme and donated the scans to Wikimedia Commons. CC0 asks for no attribution; it is given here so each plate can be traced.

The bundled files are one-bit reductions of those scans, cropped to the plate. `fallback.png` was drawn by `build_plates.py` and is part of this repository's own source.

The Met's Open Access CSV was checked for the public-domain flag of every accession listed in `catalogue.json`.
